02

Why establish a whole-school vaping initiative now?

Adolescent e-cigarette use represents one of the most rapidly escalating public health concerns across Australian secondary schools. Establishing a structured, whole-school health intervention is an urgent educational priority for three key reasons:

Epidemiological urgency & commercial determinants

Over 30% of Australian secondary students aged 14–17 have tried vaping, driven by deceptive flavourings, social media and high-dose nicotine products.

01

Failure of punitive surveillance & peer norms

Zero-tolerance measures lead to student alienation, misperceived peer norms, and do not reduce nicotine dependency.

02

Theoretical foundation

Drawing on socio-ecological theory and self-efficacy models, health behaviour change requires a whole-school, multi-system approach.

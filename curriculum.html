Section 5 / Curriculum alignment & pedagogical mapping

Victorian Curriculum mapping

Explicit alignment of Project Unclouded with Victorian Curriculum F–10 across HPE, Personal and Social Capability, and Media Arts / English.

01 Curriculum architecture

Integrated curriculum framework

Whole-school health promotion is most effective when it sits inside mandated curriculum, not as an extra-curricular add-on (VCAA, 2024; WHO, 2021). Project Unclouded maps across three Victorian Curriculum F–10 areas.

HPE · Levels 7–8

Commercial determinants, norm correction and social refusal assertiveness.

Personal & Social · Levels 9–10

Wellbeing Champions lead peer initiatives and build emotional self-regulation.

Media Arts / English · Levels 9–10

Students deconstruct marketing and co-design persuasive multilingual campaigns.

02 Subject alignment cards

Health & Physical Education · Levels 7 & 8

Universal foundation in Year 7–8 HPE: commercial determinants of health, social-norm correction and refusal assertiveness (Gardner et al., 2025).

VCAA content descriptors Program application & evidence
VC2HP8P01 — Evaluate strategies and resources that manage changes and support young people’s wellbeing, including commercial determinants and substance use. Module 1, Hours 1–2: students deconstruct e-cigarette marketing and flavour targeting. Evidence: Commercial Tactics Analysis Worksheets and norm-correction infographics.
VC2H8P04 — Practise social and assertiveness skills to manage peer influence and resist immediate health challenges. Module 1, Hours 3–4: CLEAR refusal scripts and OurFutures e-learning. Evidence: peer-evaluated role-play rubrics and OurFutures logs.

03 Endorsement descriptors

Personal & Social Capability · Levels 9 & 10

Targeted leadership for Year 9–10 Wellbeing Champions: collaborate, lead peer health initiatives and build emotional self-regulation (DET, 2021; Hunter, 2023).

VCAA content descriptors Program application & evidence
VC2P10S01 — Evaluate personal strengths, leadership capabilities, and peer support roles to foster a positive school climate. Module 2, Hour 5: Champions examine peer-messaging ethics and establish the Adult Safety Review Protocol. Evidence: signed Leadership Charters.
VC2P10S04 — Plan, implement, and evaluate collaborative community initiatives that promote diversity and health literacy. Module 2, Hours 6–7: Champions co-design and run six festival stalls. Evidence: Festival Logistics Blueprints and peer feedback logs.

04 Media Arts descriptors

Media Arts & English · Levels 9 & 10

Cross-curricular application: deconstruct commercial marketing and co-design persuasive, multilingual health campaigns (Asdigian et al., 2023).

VCAA content descriptors Program application & evidence
VC2M10D01 — Produce and distribute media artworks for specific audiences using persuasive design, digital tools and ethical standards. Module 2, Hour 6: campaign posters, graphics and social templates in Canva. Evidence: finished campaign portfolios reviewed against the safety protocol.
VC2E10LA2 — Analyse how language, multimodal features and cultural translations influence community health perception. Module 3, Hour 10: multilingual parent flyers in Swahili, Arabic, Dinka, Punjabi and Hindi. Evidence: CALD showcase displays.

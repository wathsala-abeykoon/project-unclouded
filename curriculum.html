01 Curriculum architecture & rationale

Integrated Curriculum Framework

Whole-school health promotion achieves maximum efficacy and sustainability when directly embedded within mandated curriculum structures rather than delivered as isolated, extra-curricular add-ons (Victorian Curriculum and Assessment Authority [VCAA], 2024; World Health Organisation [WHO], 2021).

Project Unclouded utilises a multi-disciplinary curriculum mapping matrix designed specifically for Victorian secondary schools. Aligned with the Victorian Curriculum F–10, the project integrates core learning areas to ensure rigorous academic standards while fostering student agency, health literacy, and critical thinking (VCAA, 2024):

1

Health and Physical Education (HPE) — Levels 7 & 8

Universal foundation focusing on analysing commercial determinants of health, norm correction, and social refusal assertiveness (Gardner et al., 2025).

2

Personal and Social Capability — Levels 9 & 10

Targeted leadership domain empowering Year 9–10 Wellbeing Champions to collaborate, lead peer health initiatives, and build emotional self-regulation (Department of Education, 2021; Hunter, 2023).

3

Media Arts / English — Levels 9 & 10

Cross-curricular application where students deconstruct commercial marketing tactics and co-design persuasive, multilingual health campaigns (Asdigian et al., 2023).

02 Subject alignment cards

Subject Alignment Cards

Subject Card 1

Health & Physical Education (HPE) — Levels 7 & 8

VCAA content descriptor

VC2HP8P09

investigate how media and influential people in the community impact attitudes, beliefs, decisions and behaviours in relation to health, safety, relationships and wellbeing

Program application & evidence of learning

Project Application (Module 1, Hours 1–2): Students deconstruct commercial e-cigarette marketing, flavour targeting, and digital advertising tactics. Worksheets now include an explicit prompt asking students to explain how a specific marketing message can influence a health belief or decision.

Evidence of Learning: Completed Commercial Tactics Analysis Worksheets (including marketing influence reflections) and Norm Correction Data Infographics.

VCAA content descriptor

VC2HP8P08

refine protective behaviours and evaluate community resources to seek help for themselves and others (Note: First aid/CPR excluded from scope)

Program application & evidence of learning

Project Application (Module 1, Hours 3–4): Students practice "CLEAR" refusal scripts through roleplay and e-learning, alongside a vaping-related help-seeking scenario and a comparative review of two distinct support resources.

Evidence of Learning: Peer-evaluated Refusal Roleplay Rubrics, help-seeking scenario responses, support resource comparison notes, and OurFutures logs.

Subject Card 2

Personal & Social Capability — Levels 9 & 10

VCAA content descriptor

VC2CP10S02

when and how to identify and use help-seeking and other productive coping strategies suited to different contexts; strategies for providing peer support, peer referral and empathetic communication in different contexts

Program application & evidence of learning

Project Application (Module 2, Hour 5): Year 9–10 Wellbeing Champions examine peer health leadership ethics, complete a short scenario response demonstrating empathetic communication and peer-to-adult referral steps, and establish the Adult Safety Review Protocol (approved by supervising staff).

Evidence of Learning: Signed Leadership Charters, evaluated empathetic communication scenario logs, and Safety Review safety protocol sign-off sheets.

VCAA content descriptor

VC2CP10O04

strategies for constructing and managing effective teams; when and how to evaluate collaboration and make recommendations for improvements

Program application & evidence of learning

Project Application (Module 2, Hours 6–7): Champions co-design and facilitate six interactive Unclouded Festival stalls. Festival blueprints explicitly record team roles, and feedback logs include a structured reflection evaluating collaboration and recommending one teamwork improvement.

Evidence of Learning: Festival Logistics Blueprints (with team role tracking) and Peer Feedback & Collaboration Evaluation Logs.

Subject Card 3

Media Arts & English — Levels 9 & 10

VCAA content descriptor

VC2AMA10C02

select and apply production processes and use media arts concepts to construct representations and produce media arts works that communicate ideas, perspectives and meaning to an intended audience

Program application & evidence of learning

Project Application (Module 2, Hour 6): Wellbeing Champions produce anti-vaping campaign posters and digital graphics via Canva, accompanied by a brief portfolio annotation explaining their intended audience and how specific media concepts/production choices communicate their anti-vaping message.

Evidence of Learning: Finished Campaign Portfolios with design annotations, reviewed against the school safety protocol.

VCAA content descriptor

Level 9 VC2E9LY08 Level 10 VC2E10LY08

create different types of texts, written and spoken, that present a point of view and expand ideas / reflect on challenging and complex issues, integrating print, multimodal and/or digital elements for an audience (Note: Written/multimodal focus; translation/professional review required prior to distribution)

Program application & evidence of learning

Project Application (Module 3, Hour 10): Students draft student-authored English multi-modal parent flyers (making a clear point of view at Year 9 and tackling the complex issue of youth nicotine use at Year 10) alongside brief audience notes, prior to multi-lingual translation (Swahili, Arabic, Dinka, Punjabi, Hindi).

Evidence of Learning: Student-authored English multimodal draft flyers, audience consideration notes, translated CALD family engagement flyers, and community showcase displays.

03 Systemic evaluation & KPI matrix

Measuring Health Outcomes & Implementation Fidelity

To satisfy whole-school accountability standards under FISO 2.0 and demonstrate rigorous health promotion planning, Project Unclouded incorporates a comprehensive evaluation framework combining quantitative health metrics, qualitative student voice indicators, and school discipline tracking (Thomas et al., 2025).

Project Unclouded: key performance indicators (KPIs) & evaluation matrix

Evaluation domainMeasurement tool & sourceTarget outcome / benchmark
1

Health Literacy & Norms

Tier 1 Universal

Measurement tool & source

Pre/post universal student surveys (Module 1, Hours 1–4).

Target outcome / benchmark

  • Statistically significant reduction in perceived vs. actual peer vaping rates.
  • >20% increase in refusal confidence self-ratings.
2

Non-Punitive Help-Seeking

Tier 2 & 3 Support

Measurement tool & source

Secondary School Nurse and Headspace Melton booking logs.

Target outcome / benchmark

  • 25% increase in voluntary nurse self-referrals for confidential nicotine care.
3

Discipline Transformation

Whole-School Systemic

Measurement tool & source

Aurevian College Compass suspension and incident data.

Target outcome / benchmark

  • 50% reduction in vape-related suspensions via redirection to health plans.
4

Student Agency & Voice

Leadership Cohort

Measurement tool & source

Wellbeing Champions pre/post leadership self-reflection.

Target outcome / benchmark

  • >85% of Champions reporting high agency ("Doing With") on Hunter's Continuum.
5

CALD Family Partnerships

Community Ecosystem

Measurement tool & source

Hour 10 Showcase attendance logs and bilingual surveys.

Target outcome / benchmark

  • >100 CALD family members attending showcase event.
